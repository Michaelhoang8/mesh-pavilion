Diagnostics

Genus describes the indexed combinatorial surface only when it is manifold and orientable. Signed volume is shown only for closed, consistently oriented, nondegenerate components. Neither result proves that the geometry is free of self-intersections.

The preview uses orthographic projection and a painter’s depth order. Intersecting triangles can be drawn in the wrong order. Rotation and zoom change only the view.
